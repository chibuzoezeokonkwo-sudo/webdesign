Who we are

Over a century of connecting people, ideas and opportunity.

Established in 1897, John Holt has grown into a Nigerian group serving businesses and communities through a diverse range of products and services. Its work brings together engineering, equipment supply, project delivery and ongoing support.

Across its businesses, John Holt provides diesel and gas generators, air-conditioning and transformers, fire-safety equipment, warehousing, construction, boat building and agricultural exports. This breadth helps customers source practical solutions for different operational needs through one experienced group.

Each project starts with understanding what a customer needs to get done. John Holt teams combine product knowledge and hands-on expertise to help identify suitable equipment, coordinate delivery and provide support beyond installation.

That combination of experience and practical service connects the group’s long history with the needs of today’s organisations. Whether supporting power, cooling, safety or logistics, John Holt aims to build dependable working relationships that help customers keep moving.

Our heritage

Rooted in experience. Focused on what’s next.

Since 1897, John Holt has evolved alongside the changing needs of Nigerian commerce and industry. Its portfolio now spans power generation, cooling, fire and safety, warehousing, construction, marine services and exports.

From supplying equipment to supporting projects and facilities, the group brings together specialist businesses to serve a wide range of customer requirements. Its focus remains on dependable products, practical expertise and responsive service—while continuing to develop solutions for the future.
